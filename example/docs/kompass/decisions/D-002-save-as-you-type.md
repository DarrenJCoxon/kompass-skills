## Question

Should learners press Save, or should notes save themselves?

## Options

1. **A Save button.** Clear, but notes are lost when learners forget.
2. **Save as they type**, a moment after they pause, with a quiet "Saved" line.

## Decision

Save as they type, one second after the learner pauses.

## Why

Sessions are short and often interrupted. A forgotten Save would lose exactly the notes learners care about.

## Consequences

notes-store must accept frequent small saves, and the panel must show when a save failed.
