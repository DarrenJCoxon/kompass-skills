## Question

Notes must be close to the video without hiding it, on desktop and on a phone.

## Options

1. **Below the video.** Simple, but learners scroll away from the video to type, and on a phone the notes sit far down the page.
2. **A tab in the side panel, beside course contents.** The video stays in view while typing; on a phone the panel opens as a sheet under the video.

## Decision

A tab in the side panel (variant B). Operator (example): "B — the video has to stay in view".

## Why

Learners take notes while watching. Variant B keeps the video visible at every width and reuses the existing side panel, so there is no new layout to learn.

## Consequences

lesson-workspace gains a Notes tab beside Contents. The phone layout needs a sheet that never covers the video.
