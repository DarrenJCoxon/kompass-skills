## What this will achieve

Teachers working through a course can write notes beside each lesson without leaving the video. They can find any note again later and download their notes for a course to keep or share with colleagues.

## Who it is for

Teachers and school staff learning at their own pace, often in short sessions between lessons, sometimes on a phone.

## Done looks like

- A learner types a note beside a lesson, leaves, comes back the next day and finds it exactly as they left it.
- A learner searches "rubric" and sees every note that mentions it, each linked to its lesson.
- A learner downloads all their notes for a course as one tidy document.
- Notes work on a phone without covering the video.

## Not in this phase

- Sharing notes with other learners inside the site.
- Notes on individual moments in a video.

## How it will work, in plain English

Notes belong to one learner and one lesson and are saved as the learner types. They sit in a panel beside the lesson, chosen by mockup, so the video stays visible. Search looks only at the learner's own notes. Downloading builds one document from the saved notes, in lesson order. Each part is a separate module with a small front door, so search and download never reach into how notes are stored.
